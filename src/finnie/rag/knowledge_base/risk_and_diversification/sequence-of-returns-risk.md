## Why *when* a downturn happens can matter as much as *whether* it happens

Two people could earn the exact same average annual return over 30 years
but end up with very different outcomes, depending purely on the *order*
those returns occurred in -- especially if they're also adding to or
withdrawing from the account along the way. This is sequence-of-returns
risk.

## Why it matters more while withdrawing than while contributing

While you're still contributing regularly (accumulating), an early
downturn is actually somewhat helpful -- you're buying more shares at lower
prices with each new contribution. But once you start *withdrawing*
regularly (typically in retirement), an early downturn is much more
damaging: you're forced to sell a larger share of a shrinking balance to
cover the same withdrawal amount, leaving less left over to recover when
the market eventually rebounds.

## A simplified illustration

Imagine two retirees, each withdrawing the same amount each year from an
identical starting balance, experiencing the exact same set of annual
returns -- but in reverse order from each other. The retiree who
experiences the market decline *early* in retirement can end up running out
of money noticeably sooner than the one who experiences the same decline
*late* in retirement, purely because of the order, even though both saw
identical average returns.

## Common ways this risk gets managed

- Holding some portion in more stable assets (bonds/cash) specifically to
  draw from during a downturn, avoiding forced stock sales at low prices.
- Flexible withdrawal strategies that reduce withdrawals during a downturn
  rather than withdrawing a fixed amount regardless of market conditions.
- Maintaining a cash buffer covering a year or more of expenses heading
  into retirement.

## Going deeper

This risk is a strong argument for shifting toward a somewhat more
conservative allocation as retirement approaches -- not to maximize
long-run average return, but specifically to reduce the damage a
poorly-timed early downturn could do during the withdrawal phase.
